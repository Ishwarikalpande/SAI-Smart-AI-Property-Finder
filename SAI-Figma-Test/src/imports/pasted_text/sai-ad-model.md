UPDATE THE SAI PROPERTY PROMOTION SYSTEM TO FOLLOW THIS EXACT BUSINESS MODEL:

SAI HAS TWO DIFFERENT SIDES OF THE ADVERTISING SYSTEM:

1. ADVERTISER SIDE:
   AGENTS AND BUILDERS PAY FOR SUBSCRIPTION PLANS TO PROMOTE THEIR OWN PROPERTIES OR APARTMENT PROJECTS.

2. USER SIDE:
   BUYERS / PROPERTY SEEKERS SEE THE RESULTING SPONSORED AND FEATURED PROPERTY CONTENT INSIDE THE NORMAL SAI USER INTERFACE.

IMPORTANT:
The Buyer does NOT purchase advertising subscriptions.
The Buyer does NOT manage advertisements.
The Buyer does NOT see advertising management or subscription billing screens.

Only Agents and Builders can purchase and manage promotion subscriptions.

==================================================
AGENT ADVERTISING
==================================================

Agents can purchase subscription plans to promote their own individual property listings.

Agent flow:

AGENT DASHBOARD
        ↓
MY LISTINGS
        ↓
SELECT MY PROPERTY
        ↓
PROMOTE THIS LISTING
        ↓
SELECT SUBSCRIPTION PLAN
        ↓
PAYMENT
        ↓
ADVERTISEMENT / PROMOTION ACTIVE

The Agent can:

- Select their own property listing
- Choose a subscription plan
- Pay for promotion
- Set or select the promotion duration according to the subscription
- View active promotions
- View advertisement performance
- View impressions
- View clicks
- View enquiries
- Renew or upgrade their subscription

The Agent cannot promote properties belonging to other Agents.

==================================================
BUILDER ADVERTISING
==================================================

Builders can purchase subscription plans to promote their own apartment projects and available units.

Builder flow:

BUILDER DASHBOARD
        ↓
MY APARTMENT PROJECTS
        ↓
SELECT PROJECT OR AVAILABLE UNIT
        ↓
PROMOTE PROJECT / UNIT
        ↓
SELECT SUBSCRIPTION PLAN
        ↓
PAYMENT
        ↓
ADVERTISEMENT / PROMOTION ACTIVE

The Builder can:

- Select their own apartment project
- Promote the entire apartment project
- Promote available apartments or units where applicable
- Choose a subscription plan
- Pay for promotion
- View active promotions
- View advertisement performance
- View impressions
- View project or unit views
- View buyer enquiries
- Renew or upgrade their subscription

The Builder cannot promote projects or properties belonging to other Builders.

==================================================
SUBSCRIPTION PLANS
==================================================

Create separate subscription plans for:

AGENTS:
Plans for promoting individual property listings.

BUILDERS:
Plans for promoting apartment projects and available units.

The subscription plans are available ONLY inside:

- Agent Portal
- Builder Portal

The subscription plans must NOT appear inside the Buyer interface.

Use Indian Rupee (₹) currency notation for pricing.

==================================================
HOW ADVERTISEMENTS APPEAR TO BUYERS
==================================================

The Buyer does not see the subscription system.

Instead, promoted content can appear naturally inside the existing SAI Buyer experience.

Promoted content may appear in:

- Home page
- Property search results
- Property listing feeds
- AI search results where relevant
- Map results where relevant
- Apartment project discovery
- Recommended property sections

Clearly label promoted content as:

- Sponsored
- Featured Property
- Featured Project
- Promoted Listing

Do not make advertisements look deceptive or pretend that paid content is an organic recommendation.

ADVERTISING ANALYTICS
==================================================

Agents should see analytics for their own promoted listings:

- Impressions
- Views
- Clicks
- Click-through rate
- Enquiries
- Leads generated

Builders should see analytics for their own promoted projects or units:

- Impressions
- Project views
- Unit views
- Clicks
- Buyer enquiries
- Leads generated

Do not expose advertiser analytics to Buyers.

==================================================
ROLE SEPARATION
==================================================

BUYER:

Sees promoted content inside the normal SAI property discovery experience.

Does not manage advertising.

AGENT:

Pays for subscription plans and promotes their own individual property listings.

BUILDER:

Pays for subscription plans and promotes their own apartment projects and available units.

Keep all existing Buyer, Agent, and Builder role separation intact.

Do not add advertising subscription management to the Buyer dashboard.

Do not allow Agents to promote Builder projects.

Do not allow Builders to manage Agent listings.
